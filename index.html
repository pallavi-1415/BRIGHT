FAQ

Questions? We've got answers.

Is treatment at BrightSmile painful?

We use a comfort-first approach: numbing gel before injections, modern minimally invasive techniques and calm, quiet rooms. Most patients tell us it was far easier than they expected.

Do you accept dental insurance?

We work with most major insurance providers and will help you understand your coverage before treatment begins. Flexible payment plans are also available.

How often should I have a check-up?

For most people, every six months is ideal. We'll recommend a schedule that suits your oral health and lifestyle.

Do you treat children?

Absolutely. Our kids' dentistry is designed to be fun and fear-free, helping little ones build healthy habits from their very first visit.

Can I book an emergency appointment?

Yes. Call us and we'll do our best to see you the same day for urgent issues such as pain, swelling or a broken tooth.
